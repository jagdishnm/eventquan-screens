# EventQuan × Texas A&M: six screens

Six PNGs at iPhone 15 Pro resolution (`01`–`06`) and the assembled journey (`00-journey-all-six.png`), in journey order: Sign in → Home → EventFinder → Kyle Field → Game day → Quick Event. Full working notes are in `NOTES-appendix.md`.

## What I did differently from the boards
- **Home:** live context sits above his twelve tiles, and only the tiles with something live show a status line, so they stand out. The grid stays.
- **Game day:** I picked the in-stadium view (live score, your seat, order to seat) as the one that proves the switch to stadium app.
- **Quick Event:** one sentence, like posting. Time, place and node are picked up automatically, then Post.

## Left out
- Messages and Profile tabs are not designed.
- A full live campus map. Kyle Field is reached from the Map tab directly.
- Quick Event's RSVP view for invitees, and step 3 of first run (interest picker).
- Game day's pre-game state (countdown, parking, gate wait).
